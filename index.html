Product Description

Brief Description

The AL buds represent a massive leap in wearable tech, packing medical-grade monitoring into a device weighing less than a wristband. In the core of the AL buds is a second generation ALbud™ sensor, a clinical-grade health monitoring sensor condensed to the size of a nickel. By shifting the sensors from the wrist to the ear, the AL buds provide users with a fundamental sensing advantage. Crafted from aluminium and high-quality ABS plastic, it blends as a casual earbud, without sacrificing both features and beauty.

What problems does it solve?

This product eliminates the key frustrations of ordinary, run of the mill health tracking devices, including inaccurate data and dead batteries. With the AL buds, the swappable battery system allows for easy, 24/7 tracking by cycling through two rechargeable batteries. As the AL bud rests on your ear, it solves the interference issues caused by wrist movements, providing more accurate health data readings at a much more affordable $2400 HKD.

Targeted Audience

  • Health conscious: Users seeking 24/7, medical-grade tracking for sleep, menstrual cycles and blood flow.
  • Style conscious: Users who want high-end health data without the look of a plastic wristband or a bulky smart ring.
  • Budget conscious: Users who want premium health features without breaking the bank for a high-end smartwatch.

How does it work?

The AL buds work by ditching the wrist for the ear, where the skin is thinner and blood flow is much easier to read. The ALbud™ sensor taps into the steady pulse near your ear canal, ensuring your data isn't messed up by arm movement. To keep it running 24/7, you simply swap the outer battery cartridge when it’s low. This modular design keeps the internal sensors active at all times, providing a seamless physiological map without ever taking a break.
